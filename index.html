Audience Intelligence

A personal analytics tool for the owner of Whispers of Battle and The Third Derivative. It connects authorized YouTube reports to documentary planning and quality review.

Read-only insight for better films

The tool retrieves channel and video information, views, watch time and audience-retention reports where available. The owner uses these reports to study openings, pacing and production choices.

It requests only read-only YouTube account and analytics permissions. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos through those permissions.

How access works

  1. The channel owner chooses a channel and grants permission through Google OAuth.
  2. Private credentials are held in GitHub Actions secrets for the authorized collector.
  3. Reports and production records are used for the owner's analysis. Recommendations require review before changing approved creative work.

Current availability

This is a personal production integration, not a public signup service. Both channel connections have passed a bounded live test. Historical backfill, unattended scheduling and some production integrations remain in development.

This information site contains no private reports, credentials, screening copies or sign-in form.
